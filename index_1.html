I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the Universities of Ferrara and Parma, and I am on the 2026–27 job market. I submitted my thesis in October 2026, and my defence is scheduled for February 2027.

I am an applied economist, trained first in the political and social sciences, working on labour markets, innovation and the green transition. My doctoral research examines how workers value the non-wage green attributes of their jobs, whether firms’ green innovation delivers measurable environmental improvement, and how geographical and cultural origin shapes the sorting of inventors across firms. Before my doctorate, I studied the labour-market integration of migrants with qualitative methods.

I combine experimental methods (discrete choice experiments, survey design) and microeconometrics (causal inference, difference-in-differences, panel data) with qualitative fieldwork (ethnography and interviews). During my PhD, I was a visiting PhD researcher at the Bordeaux School of Economics and at Imperial College London.
